# Visual checklist

What to look at in Roblox Studio, in order, the first time you see the environment. Roughly
20 minutes. Work top to bottom — each section assumes the one above looked right.

Set up first: [PC_SYNC_GUIDE.md](PC_SYNC_GUIDE.md) → connect Rojo → press **Play**.
Keep **View → Output** open. Press **F3** for the part counter and **F4** for the route gizmo
(both Studio-only).

If something is wrong, note the section number and grab a screenshot — that is enough for me to
find it.

---

## 1. Lighting and mood (lobby, before you start)

| # | Look at | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1 | The room overall | Shadows lean **teal/cold**; the only warm light is the ghost light and the stage spots. Not grey, not washed out. |
| 1.2 | Screen edges | A soft dark **vignette**, strongest in the corners. Subtle — you should notice it only if you look. |
| 1.3 | Depth | A light haze with distance. Far walls are slightly veiled, not crisp. |
| 1.4 | The ghost light bulb | Blooms gently. A soft halo, not a starburst. |
| 1.5 | Output | `[Main] Don't Look Backstage ready: 3 stages`, no red errors, no `[MapBuilder] stage N layout:` warnings. |

## 2. The story props (still in the lobby)

| # | Look at | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.1 | Back wall, left | **Playbill** for "THE UNDERSTUDY" on a red board, listing ACT I/II/III — the three stage names. |
| 2.2 | Back wall, right | **Cast list**. `LEAD … R. HOLLOWAY` struck through; `UNDERSTUDY … PINE` in red. |
| 2.3 | Right-hand wall | **Countdown**: a big `0`, with `5 4 3 2 1` crossed out below, and `PLACES, PLEASE`. |
| 2.4 | Wardrobe rail | Four **tags on strings**. Read them; the last says `UNDERSTUDY / PINE`. |
| 2.5 | The floor | Faint **footprints** leading from the rehearsal stage toward the stage door. |
| 2.6 | On the rehearsal stage | A few **script pages** lying flat, each with a readable line. |
| 2.7 | All text | Readable without zooming. Nothing clipped or overflowing its board. |

## 3. Act I — The Empty Auditorium

| # | Look at | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3.1 | Turn and look at the house | Curved rows of **velvet seats** sweeping away into mist. Individual seats with backs and armrests — not solid blocks. |
| 3.2 | Variety | Some seats tipped up, some faded/duller. Rows should **not** look copy-pasted. |
| 3.3 | The aisles | Red runners in **separate torn pieces** with gaps, not one clean strip. |
| 3.4 | House-left wall, high | **Three windows with shafts of moonlight** angling down into the seats, with dust drifting inside them. The shafts land *behind* the platforms, never across your jumps. |
| 3.5 | Front row, centre | **One empty seat** under a narrow warm spot. |
| 3.6 | Toward the stage | A **proscenium arch**: fluted pillars, gathered red curtains, a valance, a brass star at the top. |
| 3.7 | The stage floor | Visible **planks** in slightly different shades, with **footprints** in the dust leading off. |
| 3.8 | Above the stage | A **fly loft**: battens, pulleys, ropes, and a few hanging **sandbags**. |
| 3.9 | House wall | The **rehearsal schedule** board — every day struck off but the dress rehearsal. |
| 3.10 | The route itself | Every platform is clearly **lighter and warmer** than the scenery, with **glowing tape** on the edges you jump from. |

## 4. Act II — The Painted Street

| # | Look at | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4.1 | The facade | Flat painted shopfronts: framed windows, **striped awnings**, cornices under the roof lines. |
| 4.2 | The windows | Warm **glowing** panes on two floors with cross bars. Two cast real light onto the street. |
| 4.3 | Above | A **painted moon** with a ring and craters. |
| 4.4 | The roofline | **Chimneys with smoke** drifting up. They must be on the *facade*, never on the roofs you run across. |
| 4.5 | The ladder | **Glow bands** at its foot and its head. |
| 4.6 | Roofs | Clear and uncluttered — nothing standing on them. |
| 4.7 | The flats | A **wet paint** notice ("the moon is not dry yet") and a playbill. |
| 4.8 | The slide mat | Obviously blue and distinct, and you slide **toward the landing**, not back toward the roofs. |

## 5. Act III — Curtain Call

| # | Look at | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5.1 | The staircase | Cardboard steps with **packing tape and stencils**. Step on one: the dressing falls **with** the step. |
| 5.2 | Below the stairs | Curved rows of **empty audience seats**. |
| 5.3 | The far end | **Two beams narrowing** toward the two doors. |
| 5.4 | The floor | **Confetti and torn paper** scattered on the stage and in the pit. |
| 5.5 | The two doors | Thick moulded frames, gilt casing, a keystone. Only **one has light beneath it**; the other looks flat and painted-on. |
| 5.6 | In the wings | The **prompt book** open on a stand — last cue blank. Clear of your run to the doors. |

## 6. The theatre is alive

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6.1 | Stand still 20 seconds | Curtains breathe. Sandbags swing slowly. Lamps flicker now and then. |
| 6.2 | Run along a curtain | It **ripples** as you pass, then settles. |
| 6.3 | Walk across a stage floor | Occasional **creak + puff of dust** underfoot. It should tick, not rattle. |
| 6.4 | Let the mannequin get close | Lamps flicker **harder and more often**, with an audible buzz when it is very close. |
| 6.5 | Pass a brass-star door | Handle turns, strip lights under the door, a **pool of warm light widens on the floor**, then it opens. |

## 7. Scares (each is captioned first)

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7.1 | Act I, centre stage | A spotlight comes up, your **shadow bows** on the curtain, then the mannequin bows behind you. |
| 7.2 | Act II, first roof | A figure walks past the **upstairs windows**, then a **street lamp dies**. |
| 7.3 | Act III, stage floor | One **knock**, then **applause**, seats bouncing, and a **spotlight snaps onto each door**. |
| 7.4 | Settings → Reduced scares, replay | Every snap becomes a soft fade. No shake, no flash. **Captions stay.** |

## 8. Performance and readability

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8.1 | Press **F3** | Counter appears. **Parts well under 12,000** (about 3,200) and green, not red. FPS near 60. |
| 8.2 | Press **F4** | A wireframe box around every platform. Walk the route — **no decoration should sit inside a box or in the air between them**. |
| 8.3 | Settings → Graphics → **Low** | Light shafts and beams disappear, particles stop, curtains go still. The **route stays just as readable**. |
| 8.4 | At Low, trigger the Act II scare | The street lamp **still dies**. Scares are story, not effects. |
| 8.5 | Settings → **High** | Everything returns. |
| 8.6 | Rejoin | Your graphics choice is remembered. |

## 9. Mobile (Test → Device → iPhone SE, then a tablet)

| # | Check | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9.1 | Default graphics | Auto should pick **Low** on a touch device. |
| 9.2 | Frame rate | Steady; **View → Stats** near 60. |
| 9.3 | The route | Platforms and glow tape still read clearly at phone size against all the decor. |
| 9.4 | Text | Lore boards readable; HUD clear of the notch and the Roblox bar. |
| 9.5 | The settings panel | Fits on screen; the four Graphics buttons are easy to tap. |

---

## If something looks wrong

- Note the section number.
- Screenshot it.
- Check Output for red lines and copy the first one.
- For a misplaced prop, **F4** shows whether it is inside the route — that is the difference
  between "ugly" and "breaks the game".
